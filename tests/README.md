# Demo regression checks

The CV-led contract supersedes the earlier single-response assessment contract. Existing results in results/ cover the previous revision and are historical, not validation of this revision.

Run in fresh conversations with the revised skill. Do not reuse the development conversation: old instructions and answers contaminate the comparison. Run each case twice before a public demo. Record model, date, available tools, actual responses, and failures. Headings alone do not constitute a pass.

## Natural-entry acceptance test (required for demo)

Start with only: “I moved to the US and need to find work. I’m not sure what I could do here.” Optionally attach a CV. Do not mention stages, uncertainty rules, experiments, or research strategy. Answer normal questions with ordinary facts and preferences. If a target is selected and usable experience is present, expect the complete assessment without workflow coaching. Fail if the tester must ask the assistant to advance stages or stop interviewing. Record all turns. This case has not yet been verified in a fresh desktop session.

## Cases

1. **Target plus experience:** Business Operations Manager, Austin, managed ten support specialists, allocated work, controlled quality, improved workflow, approximate customer feedback. Expect complete four-part initial analysis, with no demand for exact metrics. Missing budgets are unknown, not a proven deficit.
2. **Sparse/informal evidence:** Project coordination in Spain; organized volunteer shifts and supplies; no CV. Expect provisional analysis, relevant evidence, no invented education or employment, practical test. Repeat with research unavailable.
3. **Discovery then selection:** Enjoy organizing community events, dislike sales, no direction. After initial exploration select project coordination. Expect target assessment without restarting intake. If instead declining selection, expect a comparison experiment, not forced hiring analysis.
4. **Continuation and recovery:** After an assessment and proposed evidence-summary draft, say OK. Expect the draft. Then correct ten reports to six and report a vacancy closed; request another. Expect updated evidence, retained target, removed lead and replacement research or an explicit unavailable-research fallback.

## Pass criteria

- Uploaded CV is read first; skills, background, experience and document coverage are summarized briefly. Currentness, intent/direction and market questions are bundled only when missing.
- Market analysis follows intake; meaningful options precede a user hypothesis choice when the direction is unknown. An already selected target requires no repeated selection.
- Once the hypothesis is established, calibration and a lightweight plan follow without repeated optional evidence questions.
- Responses stay short with clear subheadings (or bold labels under higher-priority formatting constraints).
- Final brief is offered once and created after acceptance; it retains preferences, market, hypothesis, relevant options, calibration and next steps.
- Market lens describes responsibilities, requirements and variation; sources are relevant or limitations explicit.
- Recruiter and hiring-manager interpretations reference evidence and requirements.
- Gaps distinguish absent evidence from absent capability; priorities are justified.
- One primary action addresses a finding and has timeframe, output and review criteria.
- Feasibility and work preferences remain separate.
- No fabricated experience, confirmed eligibility, metric precision, or active vacancies.
- Corrections update downstream findings without resetting unrelated context.
- A narrow follow-up does not repeat the entire report.

## Evidence limits

Reports in results/ are bounded agent simulations using the actual skill files. They are useful smoke checks, not fresh desktop end-to-end runs, repeated-run benchmarks, or proof of reliability for every user. Live browser/application verification needs a separate rehearsal. Do not claim eight production runs passed unless they were actually performed.
