# Stage 3: Role Gap Calibration

Compare user evidence with the target's market expectations and any hiring simulation performed. Stage 2 and current openings are optional inputs. For multiple directions, keep findings distinct; do not combine different requirements into one impossible candidate profile.

Classify only material findings:

- **Transferable strength:** relevant capability supported by evidence.
- **Competency gap:** a capability the user needs to develop, supported by more than an omitted keyword.
- **Evidence gap:** a capability the user may have but cannot yet demonstrate.
- **Positioning gap:** relevant experience expressed in language the target audience may not recognize.
- **Exposure gap:** limited opportunity to experience or validate the work.
- **Hiring uncertainty:** an unresolved screening question, not a capability judgment.
- **Access constraint:** a specific opportunity condition to verify or accommodate, separate from skill.

When evidence is insufficient to determine whether a capability exists, record an evidence gap or an open question rather than asserting a competency gap. Avoid scoring missing experience as a personal deficit.

## Output

| Finding | Type | Evidence or uncertainty | Priority and rationale | Next implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use high, medium, or low priority based on role importance, evidence strength, and relevance to the user's experiment. For each material finding, state whether to build, demonstrate, explain, verify, or take no action. Summarize the practical distance to the target without a numerical fit score or a repeated list of all table entries.
