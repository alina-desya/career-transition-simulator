# Stage 3: Role Gap Calibration

Compare the confirmed user evidence with the market lens and hiring simulation. Do not treat every missing keyword as a skill deficit.

Classify material findings as:

- **Transferable strength:** relevant capability supported by evidence
- **Competency gap:** capability the user needs to develop
- **Evidence gap:** capability the user may have but cannot yet demonstrate convincingly
- **Positioning gap:** relevant experience expressed in language the target audience may not recognize
- **Exposure gap:** insufficient access to people, environments, or opportunities needed to validate the transition
- **Hiring uncertainty:** a question that may arise but is not a capability judgment

Assign high, medium, or low priority based on frequency in market evidence, importance to role outcomes, and probable hiring impact. Explain the rationale without implying false numerical precision.

Then summarize:

End by answering: **What is the real distance between my current profile and the target role?** Then pause for review.

After the user approves the stage, naturally offer to save the gap review for later. Do not mention internal filenames. Include the terminology or positioning translations that matter, the calibrated findings, priorities, assumptions, and the user's corrections. Offer Markdown, Word, or PDF when supported, then ask whether they want to continue to the transition plan or pause.
