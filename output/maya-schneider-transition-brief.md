# Career Transition Brief

Historical fictional demo, not a record of a complete simulation. This scenario assumes Maya explicitly chose the US market and an application experiment; those preferences must not be inferred from a CV address. The links and review date below are retained from the original example and have not been reverified. Work authorization remains unresolved, so these are conditional leads rather than a confirmed actionable shortlist.

## Experiment

Test whether an experienced international SaaS customer-support and care professional can transition into US implementation and customer-onboarding roles. The experiment is designed to measure employer response after the experience is repositioned using US-market implementation language.

## Selected path

**Primary path:** SaaS implementation / customer onboarding

**Adjacent paths:** customer enablement, technical support leadership

The primary path is supported by experience with customer migrations, product training, troubleshooting, escalations, cross-functional coordination, and leadership of a 10-person customer-care team.

## Next actions

1. Reposition the CV and LinkedIn profile around implementation, onboarding, migration, go-live, training, documentation, and stakeholder coordination.
2. Add measurable details to two or three examples: customer volume, project duration, complexity, team size, response-time improvement, adoption, or other outcomes where accurate.
3. Apply selectively to 10–15 Austin or US-remote implementation, onboarding, customer-enablement, and technical-support roles.
4. Prepare five concise English interview stories covering a migration, difficult customer issue, technical escalation, coaching example, and process improvement.
5. Confirm US work authorization and exclude roles whose stated requirements conflict with it.

## Success signals

- Recruiters describe the profile as relevant to implementation or onboarding.
- At least two or three recruiter screens result from 10–15 targeted applications.
- Interviewers understand the scope of prior migration and coordination work.
- Feedback identifies a specific improvement rather than a general concern about international experience or English.

## Main uncertainties

- Whether employers will recognize prior work as full implementation ownership rather than customer support.
- Whether English communication evidence is strong enough in written applications and interviews.
- Whether US work authorization limits the available employer pool.
- Whether a support-lead or customer-enablement role may provide a faster bridge into the US market.
